# Business Model

## Free tier

Beginner content, story campaign, daily challenges, limited labs.

## Pro tier

Advanced labs, higher lab limits, advanced campaign missions, AI mentor,
advanced assessments, detailed analytics.

## Cosmetics

Avatars, outfits, terminal themes, profile borders, HQ decorations.

## The one hard rule

> Never sell gameplay power.

Nothing purchasable increases XP gain, unlocks shortcuts around skill
prerequisites, or otherwise advantages a paying player over a free one in
actual challenge-solving. Pro buys access breadth and tooling (labs,
analytics, AI mentor); cosmetics buy status only. This is the same principle
behind [Achievements](./03-progression-ranks-skills.md) granting cosmetics,
not power.
